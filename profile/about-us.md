## Technology & Engineering

We work with modern technologies and engineering practices to build reliable,
maintainable, and scalable solutions.

- **Frontend:** React, Next.js, TypeScript
- **Backend:** Node.js, serverless architectures
- **UI:** Tailwind CSS, shadcn/ui
- **Database & Infrastructure:** Firebase, PostgreSQL
- **Integrations:** REST APIs, webhooks, third-party services
- **Deployment:** Cloud-based hosting and CI/CD workflows

*Our technology choices depend on the requirements of each project.*
